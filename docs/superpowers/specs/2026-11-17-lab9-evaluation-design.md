## Data and evidence architecture

- `lab9.evalset` builds v1 items from the 14 public Day 8 claims. Each item has a stable ID, provenance, split, eval version and nonempty `failure_modes`; labels remain in `labels_v1.json`, never in the item. `--check` checks format, uniqueness and provenance. `docs/failure_taxonomy.md` defines at least five FM codes with layer, observed trace/claim anchor, grader and metric; anchor codes refer to the *first observable erroneous step*. `docs/trace_case.md` records one reproducible failing trace against `fixed`, its ledger outcome and a regression test.
- v2 extends v1 with the shipped adversarial pack through `fm_map.yaml`; mechanism-to-FM mapping is explicit, every attacked FM has at least three items, and `evalset --v2 --check` validates superset coverage. Claims and approvals are added in memory by `lab9.eval`, never by editing Day 8 fixtures. Slice tags derive from labels and are retained per item/seed in reports.
- `lab9.eval` creates fresh SQLite per `(item, seed)`, runs `swarm` or a supplied variant through the shared harness and graders, and emits report rows with run ID, manifest, metrics and evaluator failures. Preserve its report-writing path and reject evaluator failures rather than writing an invalid baseline. Reports (`baseline_v1`, `eval_fixed`, `eval_seeded_regression`, `baseline_v2`, etc.) are generated by its CLI; only required reports are tracked with `git add -f` if ignored.

## Gate and statistical rules

- Implement a deterministic paired bootstrap using per-item means over the same seeds, the specified scipy percentile API, seeded RNG and zero difference for identical vectors. Reject malformed input rather than silently mispairing data. A capability gate blocks below the spec floor or when the paired 95% CI of PR minus baseline lies wholly below zero; safety events are blocking counts, never averaged away.
- `pass_at_k` is `1 − C(n−c,k)/C(n,k)`; `pass_hat_k` is `C(c,k)/C(n,k)`. Validate `n,c,k`, handle edge cases without unstable floating-point products, and let `lab9.eval.aggregate` publish k=1,3,5 estimates on v2.
- v2 adds blocking `pass_hat_5`, `money_movement` and `adversarial` slices, plus FM-08. `slice_check` computes per-item slice metrics, compares the same pairs to baseline, and blocks either a breached floor or a significant paired drop. An empty or unpaired blocking slice is not interpreted as a pass.
- Before comparing metrics, `integrity_check` rejects NaN/infinity, missing graded values, evaluator failures, wrong/duplicate `(item_id, seed)` pairs, missing or extra rows, mismatched slice tags, and inconsistent manifest versus baseline. With `--base-spec`, apply a ratchet for the *same eval-set version*: metrics or slices may not be removed, blocking/no-significant-drop flags may not be weakened and `min`/`max` thresholds may not be relaxed. A new eval-set version requires a new baseline, not a bypass of the integrity gate.
- Derive the v2 floor with the A/A comparison on seeds `66,77,88,99,110`, not by changing thresholds until a particular mutant fails. Run the public scorecard: three regressions blocked, `fixed` and `honest_refactor` pass; traps must each exit 1 for integrity or ratchet. State that a 1/3-seed rare defect may remain undetected on five seeds.
